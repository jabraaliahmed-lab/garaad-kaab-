Understand what’s behind the label.

Shariah screening is more than a green tick. It can involve a company’s main business activities and financial measures, assessed using a stated methodology. Standards can differ, and results may change over time.

  • Review the company’s core business activity
  • Understand financial ratios and their limits
  • Check the methodology and review date
  • Seek qualified guidance when uncertain
